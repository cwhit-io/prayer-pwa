# Voiceover — Fort Wayne Prays explainer

Read at a measured church pace (~140 words/minute). Pause at the blank lines. About **390 words** → roughly **2:45** with picture breathing.

Edit in this file. Bracketed stage notes are not read.

---

**[0:00 — campaign open]**

Fort Wayne Prays is a church-wide campaign from Blackhawk Ministries.

We are asking God for one million minutes of prayer.

Your Kingdom come in Fort Wayne, as it is in heaven.

We pray for the future God is forming. For our families. For wise, generous hearts with money. And for friends who need to know Jesus.

**[0:25 — anyone can start]**

You can start today at fortwayneprays.org.

You do not need an account. Guest prayer can still count toward the church total.

**[0:40 — timer]**

Tap Start praying. Then tap Start Timer.

Pray as long as you like. The timer keeps counting even if your screen sleeps.

When you are finished, pause, and save. Time is recorded in whole minutes.

Already prayed in the car, or around the table? You can add completed time without starting another timer.

**[1:15 — guided prayer]**

If you want a path to follow, choose Start guided prayer.

It walks through ACTS: Adoration. Confession. Thanksgiving. And Supplication. The same timer runs the whole way.

**[1:40 — sign in, pledge, pulse]**

When you are ready to keep a history, sign in with your email or your phone. You will get a one-time code.

Then you can make a campaign pledge — a weekly pace the app turns into your campaign commitment.

Prayer counts toward the church whether you pledge or not.

The public Prayer Pulse shows church-wide totals. It does not show your name, or your amount.

**[2:10 — people]**

Signed in, you can name Four Friends — a private list, so you can pray that each one would know Jesus.

They are not notified.

You can pray for someone in your household, or your church family, the same way. Their name is carried into the timer.

**[2:32 — requests]**

If you have a need, share a prayer request with the signed-in community — or keep it private with church leaders.

**[2:48 — close]**

That is it.

Open fortwayneprays.org. Tap Start praying. And add your minutes.

If you get stuck, Help and how it works is on the site.
